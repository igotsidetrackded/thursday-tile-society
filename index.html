League Guidelines

Game Play

  • Games kickoff at 7pm and we will play at least 4 rounds per evening.
  • We will play NMJL rules for American Mahjong.
  • We will play tables of 3 or 4, allowing Bob to play where needed; if we don't have enough players, we will rotate.
  • If we don't have more than two players, we will cancel the week; we will only play with a minimum of three.
  • This is a friendly league and we encourage new players; we will not be keeping score..... yet 😝

Host Responsibilities

  • Provide the space, tables, and chairs for a set number of tables (please note your capacity on the signup sheet).
  • Let us know on the signup sheet if you need other players to bring extra Mahjong sets.
  • Add your address to the address tab here with any notes on how to find your place or get in.
  • Add any notes on the schedule that players should be aware of (playing outside, pets, etc).
  • Review members per week and will inform the group if week is cancelled or moved based on number of players.

Guest Responsibilities

  • Sign up no later than Tuesday for games that week.
  • Bring your own 2026 NMJL Card or reach out to the host to secure one.
  • Eat dinner before or after, hosts may have some snacks, but do not assume.
  • BYOB if you want.
  • Notify host of your level of play (league is beginner friendly).
